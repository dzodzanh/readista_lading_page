What’s inside

A calmer way to keep reading

No feeds, no noise. Just your books, your minutes and a shelf that grows every time you finish one.

Today

Pick up right where you left off

Your current book, today’s minutes and your streak live on one quiet screen. One tap starts a session; another logs a day you read away from your phone.

  • A daily goal from 5 minutes to 3 hours
  • A yearly challenge that tells you if you’re on pace
  • “I read today” for paper-and-couch days

Sessions

Quiet sessions, no pressure

Set a timer or let it run open-ended. When you’re done, note the page you reached and how it felt — Readista does the math on pages and pace.

  • Live on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Keeps counting if you switch apps
  • Little celebrations for streak milestones

Your books

Every book gets its own page

Snap the spine so the shelf shows your copy, and the page edges for the book’s own page. Keep notes and favorite lines, and see when you’ll finish at your current pace.

  • Add books by search, barcode or by hand
  • Photo notes of pages, cropped and straightened
  • A finish-date forecast from your real pace

Insights

Your year, in books

Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly views of your minutes, pages, days read and books finished — with a calendar made of the covers you read.

  • A month calendar of covers and a year heatmap
  • Streaks, longest runs and books per year
  • Story-sized share cards for your month or a finished book

Themes

Eight rooms for your books

From light oak to Y2K chrome. First Edition is free; Readista Pro unlocks the other seven rooms and décor like a lamp or a sleeping cat — then share your shelf as a story-sized card.

Widgets
